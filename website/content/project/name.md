Dexaflow started in Brazil, every line of it, from the first commit.

It was born as **Leoflow**. The *flow* part orchestrates workflows. The *Leo*
part honors **Leonardo** ([@leonardo-jas](https://github.com/leonardo-jas)), an
architect with deep roots in Brazil's tech industry who taught its creator to
think in **state machines**: states, transitions, invariants, the things that
must never happen. The core of this project turned out to be exactly that, a
scheduler that is, underneath, a state machine.

In v0.5.0 the project took the name of the company that builds it, DexaData,
and became **Dexaflow**. The dedication stays, and so does everything people
adopted under the old name: the `leoflow` command, `leoflow.yaml`, the
`LEOFLOW_*` variables, `~/.leoflow`, the `leoflow-*` images and the
`charts/leoflow` chart all keep working. The full list, and the internal names
that keep the old spelling, is in
[names from before the rename](/reference/configuration/#names-from-before-the-rename).
